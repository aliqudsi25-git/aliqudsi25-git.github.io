About Me

Profile

I grew up between countries and languages — fluent in English, Arabic, and Farsi, and conversational in spoken Hindi — and finished high school at GEMS American Academy, playing varsity soccer alongside a full course load.

Before engineering, I spent two years in my family's confectionery in Doha. That job had two halves: measuring to tight tolerances on the production side, and negotiating face-to-face with customers on the sales side. Both turned out to be useful training. Then two years studying economics at Stony Brook, which is where the interest in how things get funded and justified came from.

I'm now at Brookdale in engineering science on a civil pathway, heading toward a B.S. in civil engineering with a structural concentration. The through-line across everything here is that I like problems where the structure and the business case both have to hold up.
